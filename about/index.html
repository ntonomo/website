about

i'm a college student with way too much free time on my hands. many people know me for making youtube videos, but i do it very sporadically and just about whenever i feel like it. when it comes to things outside of youtube, i tend to collect records, even though i suck at it. i also like cats; just about a healthy amount, i think.

i was born in washington state and lived there for the first decade of my life before moving to germany, where i spent my developing years. i speak both english and german, and i would consider stuttgart, germany my home. however, lately, i've settled in new jersey to pursue my post-secondary education.

having been deeply rooted in washington culture since my youth, i have a particular affinity for sports. i am a fan of the seahawks and mariners, and am a die-hard sonics fan even in their absence.

in addition to my love of sports (other than white-ass hockey), i love things that show loyalty to the late 2000's / early 2010's internet. i support the idea of and right to free information. however i do also have further, more radical ideas that wouldn't end well for me, but you can probably guess what they are :3

i'm a very introverted, solitary person for the most part, so while i do interact just fine and dandy with friends, it can be difficult to interact with new people. for this reason, i tend to avoid talking to people i don't know. nothing personal!
